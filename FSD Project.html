NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What is a currency converter?

A currency converter calculates the value of one currency in another using an exchange rate.

Are the exchange rates live?

ForexFlip retrieves the latest available reference rates from the Frankfurter API. Rates are updated periodically, not every second.

Can I convert different currencies?

Yes. Select your preferred currencies from the dropdown menus and enter the amount you want to convert.

Is ForexFlip free?

Yes. This demonstration currency converter is free to use.
